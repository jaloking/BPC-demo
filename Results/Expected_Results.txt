Expected reference results for the included Solomon 25-customer instances
===========================================================================

Instance       Customers    Vehicles    Total distance
-------------------------------------------------------
C101_25        25           3           191.3
R101_25        25           8           617.1
RC101_25       25           4           461.1

Notes
-----
1. These are the commonly reported reference values for the Solomon 25-customer
   subinstances formed by keeping the depot and customers 1 through 25.
2. Distances are Euclidean and the reported objective is rounded to one decimal.
3. A small last-digit difference may occur if an implementation rounds every arc
   before summation instead of rounding only the final route-set distance.
4. The values above are public benchmark reference values.

Data source
-----------
The customer data were taken from the public PyVRP/VRPLIB mirror of the original
Solomon VRPTW instances:
https://github.com/PyVRP/VRPLIB/tree/main/tests/data/cvrplib/Vrp-Set-Solomon
